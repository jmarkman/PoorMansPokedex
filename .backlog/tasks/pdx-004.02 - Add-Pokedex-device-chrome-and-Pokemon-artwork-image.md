## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Add three purely aesthetic RowDefinitions to MainWindows Grid: one at the very top, one immediately below it, and one at the very bottom, visually referencing https://archives.bulbagarden.net/media/upload/thumb/3/37/RG_Pok%C3%A9dex.png/730px-RG_Pok%C3%A9dex.png. None of these rows have functional behavior. Top row: four circles laid out left to right - a large teal circle with a thick white border, a small red circle positioned near the large circles top-right corner, a yellow circle to the right of the red one, and a dark green circle to the right of the yellow one. Second row: an Image control spanning both columns, centered horizontally and vertically, bound to PokedexViewModels image source property, which is populated from the searched PokedexEntrys official artwork front_default sprite URL. Bottom row: a black circle in the upper-left corner, a dark green rectangle with two smaller rectangles of matching length layered on top of it, and a large plus-shaped directional pad. Depends on the data rows subtask so the full set of RowDefinitions is finalized together, and on PokedexEntrys Sprites model for the artwork URL.
